If you are in immediate danger or considering self-harm, please contact local emergency services right now. Haven is peer support and not a substitute for professional help.

How it works

  • Create an anonymous post in a category.
  • Others reply supportively (also anonymous).
  • Report content that feels unsafe or inappropriate.
  • Edit/delete your own posts and replies anytime.

Community guidelines

  • Be kind and supportive.
  • Avoid personal identifying info.
  • No harassment, hate, or threats.
  • Use “Report” to flag issues.
